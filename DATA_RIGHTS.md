# Data and research rights

The MIT License covers the example code and repository-authored documentation. It does not grant rights over data returned by Panta or text originating from employers and other sources.

No employer advert corpus, applicant information or personal workspace data is bundled in this repository. The notebook retrieves published aggregate research and reproduces its arithmetic. Consult the relevant report and its reuse terms before redistributing data. Public API availability is not a blanket open-data license.

Keep the original collection date, cohort, denominator and limitations with a reused finding. The 4 November 2026 workload investigation concerns recorded title/employment-field signals, not national vacancy prevalence, confirmed schedules or hiring outcomes.
